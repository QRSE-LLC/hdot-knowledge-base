# 109 — Measurement and Payment

**Type:** Division
**Division:** Division 100 — General Provisions
**Section:** 109

---

## Summary

Section 109 governs how quantities are measured, how the Contractor is paid, and the rules for pricing extra work. It establishes the schedule of agreed prices for lump sum items, monthly progress payment mechanics, payment for materials on hand, subcontractor prompt payment obligations, withholding authority, and final payment requirements. The force account provisions in 109.06 are extensive and critical — they define exactly how extra or changed work gets priced when no agreed price exists, covering labor, materials, equipment, markup, and standby rates. Section 109.05 sets the overhead and profit markup percentages that apply across all change pricing.

---

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Schedule of Agreed Prices | Contractor-submitted breakdown of lump sum bid items; used as basis for monthly progress payments |
| Progress Payment | Monthly payment based on value of work satisfactorily completed; payment is not acceptance |
| Materials on Hand | Payment for materials not yet incorporated but received, accepted, and properly stored; does not shift risk of loss to State |
| Overhead and Profit Markup | 15% on direct labor (Contractor or sub's own forces); 7% on subcontractor/tier amounts (max 3 markup tiers total) |
| Force Account | Payment method for extra work based on actual verified costs; Contractor must use State's Force Account Form daily |
| Blue Book Rate | Maximum allowable equipment rate published by Equipment Watch; Hourly Rate = [(Monthly Rate ÷ 176) × Regional Factor × Rate Adjustment] + Hourly Operating Cost |
| Standby/Idle Equipment Rate | 50% of Blue Book computed rate for equipment on standby due to State-caused delays |
| Small Tools | Equipment with purchase price ≤$500; not compensable under force account |
| Prompt Payment | Contractor must pay subcontractors within 10 days of receiving State payment for work completed |
| Final Payment | Released after final acceptance + document checklist including tax clearances, surety consent, and payroll certifications |

## Spec References

- **109.01** — Schedule of agreed prices: lump sum breakdowns; monthly percentage estimates
- **109.02** — Payment is not acceptance; Contractor retains risk of loss even after payment
- **109.03** — Measurement of quantities: US standard measure; Engineer's measurement is final unless physically contested before work progresses
- **109.04** — Full compensation: contract price covers all costs; change order constitutes full compensation
- **109.05** — Overhead and profit: 15% / 15% / 7%; maximum 3 tiers of markup
- **109.06** — Force Account (14 subsections): labor, materials, equipment, trucks, mobilization, insurance/taxes, subcontractors, reimbursable expenses, excise tax, bond
- **109.07** — Assignment of payments: requires State and surety written consent
- **109.08** — Progress payments: monthly (16th–15th cycle); minimum $2,000 threshold; Engineer may withhold for contract violations
- **109.09** — Prompt payment: Contractor must pay subs within 10 days; complaints filed with Engineer; State may withhold future payments
- **109.10** — Withholding: 5% of contract for unsatisfactory progress; deductions for LDs and other charges
- **109.11** — Final payment: requires tax clearances, surety consent, affidavit of debt payment, SPO Form-22
- **109.12** — Records retention: 3 years from final payment or FHWA final voucher, whichever is longer

## QRSE Notes

- **15% overhead and profit cap on direct labor is firm** — contractors sometimes argue for more on complex changes. The spec is explicit. The 7% cap on pass-through subcontractor work with a 3-tier maximum is also strictly enforced.
- **Force account work requires daily documentation on the State's Force Account Form, signed by the Inspector.** If an inspector fails to sign daily, the Contractor cannot be paid for that day's force account work without a dispute. Instruct inspectors to sign every day.
- **The Blue Book rate is the ceiling, not the floor.** The Contractor's actual shop/yard rate is used if lower. Equipment rental charges cannot exceed the equipment's replacement cost over the life of the contract — this cap prevents Contractor gaming on long-duration force account items.
- **Standby equipment (109.06(H))** is paid at 50% of Blue Book rate. The Contractor must notify the Engineer in writing at the start of the standby period and submit a weekly list every Monday. Missed notifications forfeit payment.
- **Payment is not acceptance (109.02).** Progress payments do not release the Contractor from defect liability or the risk of loss. This is frequently misunderstood by both contractors and State staff.
- **Prompt payment obligations (109.09) are enforceable.** Subcontractors can file complaints directly with the Engineer. The Engineer can withhold from the GC to cover unpaid subs. QRSE should verify compliance on active projects.
- Final payment requires a current tax clearance (not more than 60 days old) and SPO Form-22. Missing these documents will delay final settlement.
