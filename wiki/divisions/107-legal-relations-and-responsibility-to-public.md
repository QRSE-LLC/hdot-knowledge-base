# 107 — Legal Relations and Responsibility to Public

**Type:** Division
**Division:** Division 100 — General Provisions
**Section:** 107

---

## Summary

Section 107 governs the Contractor's legal obligations to the public, the State, and third parties during construction. It covers insurance requirements (types, minimums, and State as additional insured), permit and license obligations, working hours and overtime protocols, public safety and traffic, contractor assignment restrictions, indemnification, pollution and hazardous material response, audit rights, and — most critically for QRSE — the full dispute and claims process (107.15). Section 107.15 is the most procedurally detailed section in Division 100 and sets out the mandatory steps a Contractor must follow to preserve any claim for additional money or time.

---

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Commercial General Liability (CGL) | Required insurance at $2M combined single limit per occurrence; State and its officers are additional insureds |
| Workers' Compensation | Required for all Contractor and subcontractor employees; must comply with current Hawaii law |
| Self-Insured Retention | Contractor may retain up to 25% of CGL/auto combined single limits; must be noted on certificate |
| Night Work | Work performed between 3:30 PM and 7:00 AM of following day; requires 10 working days advance notice |
| Overtime | Work beyond 8 hours/day or on Saturday, Sunday, or State holiday; requires 3 working days advance notice |
| Claim | Contractor's formal written demand for additional contract price or time; must be numbered, certified, and submitted per 107.15(D) |
| Potential Claim Notice | Preliminary written notice required before a formal claim can be filed; is a non-waivable condition precedent |
| Disputes and Claims Process | Multi-step process: notice → records → claim submission → Engineer review → Director appeal → court |

## Spec References

- **107.01** — Insurance: types, minimums, State as additional insured, subcontractor insurance options, self-insured retention
- **107.02** — Permits and licenses: Contractor's exclusive responsibility except those designated as State's
- **107.03** — Working hours: 7 AM–3:30 PM, Mon–Fri, excluding holidays
- **107.04** — Overtime/night work: permissible with written approval; advance notice required
- **107.05** — Contractor pays State inspection overtime costs when Contractor initiates overtime/night work
- **107.06** — Public convenience: Contractor must minimize traffic/pedestrian interference
- **107.07** — Assignment: contract not assignable without written State consent
- **107.08** — Laws: Contractor must comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws
- **107.11** — Safety and accident prevention
- **107.12** — Protection of persons and property; notification requirements for incidents
- **107.13** — Pollution control; archaeological/burial site protocol: stop work, notify Engineer immediately
- **107.14** — Indemnification: Contractor defends and indemnifies State against all injury/property claims
- **107.15** — Disputes and Claims: the full claims process
- **107.16** — Hazardous materials: unknown contamination triggers DSC provisions (104.08)
- **107.17** — Audit rights: State may audit books for 3 years from final payment

## QRSE Notes

- **107.15 is the most important subsection in the specs for QRSE's contract administration work.** Read it carefully. Key hard deadlines:
  - Formal claim must be filed within 180 days of the potential claim notice, or before final acceptance — whichever comes first
  - Engineer has 60 days to decide claims <$50K; 90 days for claims >$50K
  - Director appeal must be filed within 30 days of Engineer's decision
  - Claim amount is capped at what would be allowable under 109.06 Force Account (unless Engineer adjusts method under 104.06)
- **The "written notice a condition precedent" rule (107.15(A)) cross-references multiple other subsections** (104.02, 104.03, 104.04, 104.08, 105.04, 106.05, 108.05). A Contractor who misses any of these notice triggers cannot file a valid claim.
- **Contractor pays State inspection overtime** when the Contractor initiates overtime/night work (107.05). This is often overlooked — verify in pre-construction that overtime inspection cost is accounted for in the contractor's bid.
- **Hazardous material discovery (107.16)** triggers a duty to report to multiple agencies (DOH/HEER, EPA, USCG, NRC). Document the notification sequence carefully.
- **Claim certification (107.15(D)(7))** — the Contractor must personally certify the claim under penalty of law. False claims carry serious consequences.
- The Contractor must continue performing work during all dispute stages (107.15(H)) — work stoppages over disputed money are not allowed unless the State suspends the work or terminates the contract.
