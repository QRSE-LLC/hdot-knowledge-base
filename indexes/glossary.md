# Glossary

> **Project:** QRSE Knowledge Base — Proof of Concept
> **Source:** 2005 HDOT Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-20
>
> Terms are defined as they are used in the HDOT standard specifications. Subsection references indicate where the term is formally defined or first used.

---

| Term | Definition | Section Reference |
|------|------------|-------------------|
| Addendum | Written or graphic document issued during the bidding period that modifies or interprets the bidding documents | 101.03 |
| Bad Weather Day | A day when weather or conditions prevent a minimum of 4 hours of work with the Contractor's normal work force on controlling items | 101.03 |
| Blue Book | "Rental Rate Blue Book for Construction Equipment" by Equipment Watch — reference for allowable equipment rates in force account work | 101.03, 109.06 |
| Buy America | Federal requirement (23 CFR 635.410) that steel and iron on Federal-Aid projects be American-made; de minimis exception ≤0.1% of contract cost or $2,500 | 106.11 |
| Certificate of Compliance | English-language document from manufacturer/supplier certifying that supplied material meets all contract specification requirements | 106.07 |
| Change Order (CCO) | Written order signed by the Engineer adjusting contract price, contract time, or both; may settle claims or cover multiple field orders | 101.03, 104.04 |
| Contract Documents | Full binding set: contract, solicitation, addenda, NTP, bonds, general/special provisions, specs, drawings, change orders, field orders, and written interpretations | 101.03 |
| Contract Time | Number of calendar or working days for completion; begins at NTP date; ends at final acceptance | 101.03, 108.05 |
| Critical Path | Longest logical sequence of activities determining project completion; time extensions only granted for critical path impacts | 101.03, 108.06 |
| Differing Site Condition | Subsurface or latent physical condition differing materially from contract documents (Type I) or unknown condition of unusual nature (Type II); entitles Contractor to equitable adjustment | 104.08 |
| Engineer | Highway Administrator, Highways Division, HDOT, or authorized delegate; has full Director authority over contract matters | 101.03 |
| Field Order | Written order directing a change; may establish, defer, or decline price/time adjustment; must be protested within 3 days | 101.03, 104.03 |
| Final Acceptance | Engineer's written notice that work is satisfactorily complete; ends contract time and starts guarantee periods | 108.14 |
| Float | Time between when an activity can start and when it must start; belongs to neither party; has no monetary value | 101.03, 108.06 |
| Force Account | Payment for extra/changed work based on actual verified costs of labor, equipment, and materials plus defined markups | 109.06 |
| Guarantee | One-year guarantee from final acceptance against defects in materials, equipment, or workmanship; resets for repaired defect items | 108.17 |
| HMA | Hot Mix Asphalt — plant-mixed aggregate and asphalt cement; most common HDOT pavement material | 401.01 |
| Hveem Method | HMA mix design per AASHTO T 246/T 247; min stability 37; air voids 3–5% | 401.02 |
| Inspector | Engineer's representative for inspection; cannot waive contract provisions or act as Contractor's agent | 101.03, 105.01 |
| Job-Mix Formula (JMF) | Contractor-designed mix specification for HMA or slurry seal; must be submitted and accepted before production begins | 401.02, 404.02 |
| Liquidated Damages | Predetermined daily assessment for failure to complete within contract time; deducted from contract balance | 101.03, 108.08 |
| Marshall Method | HMA mix design per AASHTO T 245; 75-blow compaction; min stability 1,800 lbs; air voids 3–5%; flow 8–16 × 0.01 inch | 401.02 |
| Material Transfer Vehicle (MTV) | Equipment transferring HMA from haul trucks to paver; required for surface course paving >1,000 tons (except Lanai) | 401.03 |
| Non-Conforming Work | Work not meeting contract requirements; must be removed or remedied at no cost to State; not waived by Inspector inaction | 105.12 |
| Notice to Proceed (NTP) | Written notice establishing the date work must begin and contract time starts | 101.03, 108.01 |
| Potential Claim Notice | Required preliminary written notice before a formal claim can be filed; non-waivable condition precedent | 107.15 |
| PG 64-16 | Performance Grade asphalt cement standard for Hawaii: high-temp 64°C, low-temp −16°C | 401.02 |
| Punchlist | Engineer-issued list of deficiencies to correct after substantial completion | 101.03, 108.13 |
| RAP | Reclaimed Asphalt Pavement — reprocessed pavement material; allowed up to 15% by weight in HMA surface/binder courses | 401.02 |
| Shop Drawings | Contractor-prepared drawings submitted for Engineer review; acceptance does not relieve Contractor of conformance responsibility | 101.03, 105.03 |
| Slurry Seal | Thin preventative surface treatment of emulsified asphalt + fine aggregate + water; not a structural repair | 404.01 |
| Substantial Completion | Status when facility is usable for intended purpose; triggers punchlist period and starts LD calculation for punchlist failures | 101.03, 108.13 |
| Superintendent | Contractor's on-site agent for all work and communications; must be present whenever work is performed | 101.03, 105.08 |
| Surety | Bonding company executing performance and payment bonds guaranteeing Contractor's performance | 101.03 |
| TSLD | Time Scaled Logic Diagram — required progress schedule format submitted in SureTrak; shows critical path and activity relationships | 108.06 |
| Unauthorized Work | Work outside approved limits or without written authority; not compensable; can be ordered removed at no cost to State | 105.12 |
| VECP | Value Engineering Cost Proposal — Contractor proposal to reduce cost without impairing function; savings shared with State on contracts >$100,000 | 105.15 |
| Working Day | Calendar day when Contractor can work 4+ hours with normal workforce; excludes weekends, State holidays, and Engineer-ordered suspensions | 101.03 |
