PART 6 — LESSONS LEARNED
==========================

(Full version in tasks/lessons.md)

1. Concept pages over division pages — concept pages answer "what do I do?" questions;
   division pages answer "what is in this section?" questions. Build more concept pages.

2. QRSE Notes are the highest-value section — every successful Q&A answer came from
   QRSE Notes bullets, not spec references. Write these as field briefings, not summaries.

3. Output filenames must match the todo.md — when a deliverable has a planned filename,
   use that exact name.

4. Cross-page gaps need inline bridges — when a fact on page A requires a fact from page B,
   add the cross-reference inline at the point of use, not just in the Related Pages footer.

5. Reflect skill runs at session end, not project end — missed this on Day 2; corrected now.
