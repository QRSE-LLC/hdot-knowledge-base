211.05
1 SECTION 211 - EXPLORATORY WORK AT STRUCTURE FOOTINGS
2
3
4 211.01 Description. This section describes performing exploratory drilling,
5 exploratory excavation, or other surface or subsurface investigation ordered by the
6 Engineer after structure excavation to footing subgrade has been completed.
7
8 211.02 Materials. None.
9
10 211.03 Construction. When subgrade or subsurface conditions at structure
11 footings are not in accordance with the contract documents, perform exploratory
12 work as ordered by the Engineer. After completion of exploratory work, backfill
13 holes or cavities in accordance with Subsection 205.03(B) - Structure Backfill or as
14 ordered by the Engineer, or both.
15
16 211.04 Measurement. The Engineer will measure exploratory work at the
17 structure footings on a force account basis in accordance with Subsection 109.06 –
18 Force Account Provisions and Compensation and as ordered by the Engineer.
19
20 211.05 Payment. The Engineer will pay for exploratory work at the structure
21 footings on a force account basis in accordance with Subsection 109.06 – Force
22 Account Provisions and Compensation. An estimated amount may be allocated in
23 the proposal schedule under ”Exploratory Work at Structure Footings”, but the actual
24 amount to be paid will be the sum shown on the accepted force account records,
25 whether this sum be more or less than the estimated amount allocated in the
26 proposal schedule.
27
28 Payment will be full compensation for the work prescribed in this section, by
29 the Engineer, and in the contract documents.
30
31 The Engineer will pay for the following pay item when included in the proposal
32 schedule:
33
34 Pay Item Pay Unit
35
36 Exploratory Work at Structure Footings Force Account
