4 207.01 Description. This section describes ditch, spillway and water channel
5 excavation, including channels for changing courses of streams. Work described in
6 this section is exclusive of other contract work, such as roadway excavation and
7 ditch, unlined gutters, and channel excavation within the roadway cross sections.

10
11 Excavated Material 203.02(A)

13 207.03 Construction. Notify Engineer 10 working days before excavating
14 ditches, spillways and channels.
15
16 Clear and grub area in accordance with Section 201 – Clearing and
17 Grubbing. Excavate ditches, spillways and channels to the alignment, grades, and
18 typical sections shown in contract documents.
19
20 When excavation is made below required grade for ditch and channel
21 bottoms, backfill and compact areas below required grade for ditch and channel
22 bottoms with suitable excavated material. When needed, place and compact backfill
23 material in uniform horizontal layers not exceeding 8 inches in loose thickness
24 before compaction. Compact each layer of backfill until a relative compaction of 90
25 percent or more is achieved, when tested in accordance with Subsection
26 203.03(C)(2) - Relative Compaction Test.
27
28 Use suitable excavated material as needed, from ditch and channel
29 excavation to construct roadway embankments, dikes, or for other purposes.
30 Unsuitable and surplus excavated material will become Contractor's property.
31 Dispose of unsuitable and surplus excavated materials.
32
33 Remove debris and unwanted material from excavated ditches and channels.
34
35 207.04 Measurement.
36
37 (A) Engineer will measure ditch and channel excavation per cubic yard in
38 accordance to contract documents.
39
40 (B) Ditch and channel excavation will be paid on a lump sum basis.
41 Measurement for payment will not apply.
42
