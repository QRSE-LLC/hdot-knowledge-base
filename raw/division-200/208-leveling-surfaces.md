208.05
1 SECTION 208 - LEVELING SURFACES
2
3
4 208.01 Description. This section describes removing irregularities in natural
5 ground surface of designated areas. Work described by this section is exclusive of
6 other contract work, such as clearing, grubbing, dressing of shoulders, and final
7 clean-up operations.
8
9 208.02 Materials. None.
10
11 208.03 Construction. Level or flatten designated areas to produce smooth
12 surface with uniform cross sections that conform to grades set by the Engineer.
13
14 208.04 Measurement. The Engineer will measure leveling surfaces on a force
15 account basis in accordance with Subsection 109.06 – Force Account Provisions
16 and Compensation and as ordered by the Engineer.
17
18 208.05 Payment. The Engineer will pay for the accepted leveling surfaces on a
19 force account basis in accordance with Subsection 109.06 – Force Account
20 Provisions and Compensation. An estimated amount may be allocated in the
21 proposal schedule under “Leveling Surfaces”, but the actual amount to be paid will
22 be the sum shown on the accepted force account records, whether this sum be
23 more or less than the estimated amount allocated in the proposal schedule.
24
25 Payment will be full compensation for the work prescribed in this section, by
26 the Engineer, and the contract documents.
27
28 The Engineer will pay for the following pay item when included in the proposal
29 schedule:
30
31 Pay Item Pay Unit
32
33 Leveling Surfaces Force Account
