5
6 (A) Type I Traffic Signal Standards. Steel Type I traffic signal standards
7 shall be furnished. Each traffic signal standard shall be furnished with anchor
8 base; uniform, continuously tapered steel shaft; anchor bolts and nuts;
9 handhole and handhole cover; and other associated hardware necessary to
10 make each traffic signal standard complete. Entire assembly shall have no
11 rough edges or surfaces, depressions, or other defects.
12
13 (1) Shaft. Shaft shall be constructed of minimum No. 10 gage
14 material from open hearth, hot rolled steel or high tensile low alloy
15 steel conforming to ASTM A 1011 or ASTM A 595, with one-piece
16 construction. Shaft length with l0-foot mounting height shall be
17 provided.
18
19 (2) Handhole. Shaft shall have reinforced opening for a handhole,
20 located approximately 9 inches above bottom surface of anchor base
21 plate. Reinforced opening shall be furnished with gasket, cover plate,
22 and non-slip fastener. Opening shall have 3-inch by 5-inch minimum
23 inside measurement. A 1/4-inch by 1-inch long bolt, with nut and
24 washer, shall be welded inside shaft opposite handhole.
25
26 (3) Anchor Base. Steel casting, steel forging, or steel plate
27 anchor base shall be integral part of standard. Anchor base shall be
28 of sufficient strength to support standard. Anchor base shall have four
29 holes, on 8-1/2 inch diameter bolt circle (6 inches square), sized to
30 accommodate anchor bolts provided. Base plate shall be secured to
31 lower end of shaft with continuous arc welds.
32
33 (4) Anchor Bolts. Anchor bolts shall have size, strength, and
34 length in accordance with manufacturer’s recommendations and shall
35 conform to Subsection 718.01 - Standard Fasteners. Anchor bolts
36 shall have "L" bends or plate washers at bottom. Each anchor base
37 shall have four anchor bolts, with leveling nut, top nut, and two
38 washers for each anchor bolt. Anchor bolts, washers, and nuts shall
39 be zinc-coated.
40
41 (5) Finish. Complete standard and other ferrous material after
42 fabrication shall be hot-dipped and zinc-coated, internally and
43 externally in accordance with AASHTO M 232 and AASHTO M 111.
44
45 (B) Type II and Type III Traffic Signal Standards. Type II and Type III
46 traffic signal standards shall accommodate mast arm mounting of traffic
47 signals, with or without bracket-mounted traffic signals.
