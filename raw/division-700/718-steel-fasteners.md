4 718.01 Standard Fasteners. Standard bolts shall conform to ASTM A 307.
5
6 Anchor bolts shall conform to ASTM A 193; anchor bolt nuts to AASHTO
7 M 292. Anchor bolts, including nuts and washers, shall be hot-dip zinc coated.
8
9 The Contractor shall submit certified test results of bolts and nuts in
10 accordance with the requirements of this subsection. Certifications shall be
11 submitted before installing bolts.
12
13 718.02 High-Strength Bolts And Studs. Bolts and studs shall conform to
14 AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications, Subsection 6.4.3.1. Bolts designated
15 as high-strength bolts shall be AASHTO M 164 structural bolts, steel, heat-treated,
16 120 or 105 kips per square inch (ksi) minimum tensile strength, Type 1. Bolts 1/2
17 inch to 1 inch in diameter shall have minimum tensile strength of 120 ksi. Bolts 1-
18 1/8 inches to 1-1/2 inches in diameter shall have minimum tensile strength of 105
19 ksi. Anchor bolts 1-3/4 inches to 3 inches in diameter and designated as high-
20 strength studs shall conform to ASTM A 449, Type 1. Bolts and studs shall be hot-
21 dip zinc coated. AASHTO M 253 bolts shall not be hot-dip zinc coated.
22
23 Bolts shall conform to requirements for heavy hexagon structural bolts in
24 ANSI B18.2, and dimensions in Table 718.02-1 – Bolt and Nut Dimensions.

32 718.03 Nuts. Nuts shall conform to AASHTO M 291. Nuts shall be hot-dip zinc
33 coated.
34
35 Nuts shall be Grade DH or 2H for use with AASHTO M 164, Type 1, bolts.
36 Nut shall be marked on one face with grade symbol DH or 2H, as appropriate.
37 Grade marking shall be impressed on bearing surface of nut.
38
39 Nuts shall conform to requirements for heavy semi-finished nuts in ANSI
40 B18.2, and dimensions shown in Table 718.02-1 - Bolt and Nut Dimensions
41 (Inches). Bearing surface of nut under bolt head shall not be less than dimensions
42 shown in Table 718.02-1 – Bolt and Nut Dimensions.
43
44 Nuts shall be tapped oversize after hot-dip zinc coating. Overtapping shall be
45 as necessary for threading of nut onto bolt when both are zinc coated. Overtapping
46 shall conform to mechanical property and rotational capacity test requirements in
47 AASHTO M 291. Overtapping threads of nuts shall not exceed the allowances in
48 Paragraph 7.4 and Table 5 of AASHTO M 291.
49
50 Zinc-coated nuts shall be lubricated with lubricant containing visible dye.
51
52 718.04 Washers. Washers shall be hardened steel washers conforming to
53 AASHTO M 293.
54
55 Hardened steel washers shall have flat and smooth surfaces and nominal
56 dimensions conforming to Table 718.04-1 – Washer Dimensions (Inches).

69 718.05 Testing.
70
71 (A) Bolts.
72
73 (1) Proof load tests in accordance with ASTM F 606, Method 1, are
74 required. Minimum frequency of tests shall be in accordance with
75 AASHTO M 164, Paragraph 10.2.4.
76
77 (2) Wedge tension tests on full-size bolts in accordance with ASTM
78 F 606, Paragraph 3.5, are required. Bolts shall be tested after zinc
79 coating. Minimum frequency of tests shall be in accordance with
80 AASHTO M 164, Paragraph 10.2.4.
81
82 (3) Thickness of zinc coating shall be measured on wrench flats or
83 top of bolt head.
84
85 (B) Nuts.
86
87 (1) Proof load tests in accordance with ASTM F 606, Paragraph
88 4.2, are required. Minimum frequency of tests shall be in accordance
89 with AASHTO M 291, Paragraph 9.3; or AASHTO M 292, Paragraph
90 7.1.2.1. Nuts shall be tested after zinc coating, overtapping, and
91 application of lubricant containing visible dye.
92
93 (2) Thickness of zinc coating shall be measured on wrench flats.
94
95 (C) Washers.
96
97 (1) Hardness of washers shall be tested after zinc coating.
98 Coating shall be removed before hardness measurements are taken.
99
100 (2) Thickness of zinc coating shall be measured.
101
102 (D) Bolt, Nut, and Washer Assemblies. Rotational-capacity tests on
103 bolt, nut, and washer assemblies are required. Assemblies shall be tested
104 after zinc coating and before shipping. Washers shall be part of test.
105 Washers may not need to be part of installation procedure.
106

718.05
107 The following shall apply:
108
109 (1) Rotational-capacity test shall be done in accordance with
110 AASHTO M 164, except as modified by this subsection.
111
112 (2) Each production lot combination of bolt, nut, and washer shall
113 be tested as an assembly. Where washers are not required for
114 installation, washers do not have to be included in lot identification.
115
116 (3) Rotational-capacity lot number shall be assigned to each
117 combination of lots tested.
118
119 (4) Minimum frequency of testing shall be two assemblies per
120 rotational-capacity lot.
121
122 (5) Bolt, nut, and washer shall be assembled on Skidmore-Wilhelm
123 Calibrator or accepted equivalent device. The above requirement
124 supersedes the requirement for the Contractor to test in steel joint as
125 specified in AASHTO M 164. Bolts that are assembled too short in
126 Skidmore-Wilhelm Calibrator shall be tested as specified in
127 Subsection 718.05(D)(9).
128
129 (6) Minimum rotation from snug-tight condition (10 percent of
130 specified proof load) shall be as follows:
131
132 (a) 240 degrees (2/3 turn) for bolt lengths up to and
133 including 4 diameters.
134
135 (b) 360 degrees (one full turn) for bolt lengths over 4
136 diameters but not exceeding 8 diameters.
137
138 (c) 480 degrees (1-1/3 turn) for bolt lengths over 8
139 diameters.
140
141 The requirements indicated above supersede values specified in
142 AASHTO M 164.
143
144 (7) Turn-test tension reached at the above rotation shall be equal
145 to or greater than 1.15 times the required installation tension.
146 Installation tension and turn-test tension shall be as shown in Table
147 718.05-3 – Installation Tension and Turn - Test Tension.

180 718.06 Documentation.
181
182 (A) Mill Test Report (MTR).
183
184 (1) MTRs shall be submitted for mill steel used in manufacturing
185 bolts, nuts, and washers.
186
187 (2) Each MTR shall show location of melting and manufacture.
188
189 (B) Manufacturer-Certified Test Report (MCTR).
190
191 (1) MCTRs shall be submitted for bolts, nuts, and washers.
192
193 (2) MCTR shall conform to the reporting requirements in
194 Subsection 718.05(E) - Reporting.
195
196 (3) MCTR shall include the following information from manufacturer
197 that performed rotational-capacity test:
198
199 (a) Lot number of each item tested.
200
201 (b) Rotational-capacity lot number, as required by
202 Subsection 718.05(D)(3).
203
204 (c) Test results, as required by Subsection 718.05(E)(1).
205
206 (d) Information required by Subsection 718.05(E)(2).
207
208 (e) Statement that MCTR for nuts, bolts, and washers
209 complies with the requirements of this Subsection 718.05 -
210 Testing and applicable AASHTO specifications.
211
212 (f) Location where components of bolt assembly were
213 manufactured.

237 718.07 Shipping.
238
239 (A) Bolts, nuts, and washers from each rotational-capacity lot shall be
240 shipped in the same container. If there is only one production lot number for
241 each size of nut and washer, nuts and washers may be shipped in separate
242 containers. Each container shall be marked permanently with its
243 rotational-capacity lot number to make identification possible at various
244 stages before installation.
245
246 (B) The MTR, MCTR, or DCTR, as appropriate, shall be submitted in
247 accordance with the contract documents.
248
249 718.08 Installation. Shop or field installation of high-strength bolts shall conform
250 to AASHTO Standard Specifications for Highway Bridges, Division II, Subsection
251 11.5.6.4, Installation, and as modified by this subsection. Bolts shall be installed in
252 accordance with AASHTO Standard Specifications for Highway Bridges, Subsection
253 11.5.6.4.7, Direct Tension Indicator Installation Method, using zinc-coated indicators.
254 The exception is that anchor bolts for railing posts base plates may also be installed
255 as specified in Subsection 11.5.6.4.4, Turn-of-Nut Installation Method, and
256 Subsection 11.5.6.4.5, Calibrated Wrench Installation Method, of AASHTO Standard
257 Specifications for Highway Bridges.
