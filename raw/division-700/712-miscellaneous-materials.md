30

32 for support of structural members. Grout shall provide the required quantity
33 of fill material without gap or void, and shall flow freely during pumping.

35
36 Grout shall have portland cement base, expansive agent, carefully
37 graded silica sand, and shall be packaged in 50-pound multi-wall bags with
38 polyethylene liners.

212 in thickness shall not be laminated. Pad thicker than 1/2 inch shall be
