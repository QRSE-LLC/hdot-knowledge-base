10 711.03 Admixtures. Admixtures in portland cement concrete shall be used when
11 indicated in the contract documents or authorized in writing by the Engineer.
12
13 Chemical and air-entraining admixtures shall not be used in prestressed or
14 reinforced concrete if chloride content is more than 1 percent by weight of
15 admixture, as determined by California Test 415.
16
17 Admixture containing calcium chloride shall not be used in concrete with steel
18 reinforcement or other embedded metal.
19
20 Admixture that is non-uniform during application shall not be used.
21
22 If two or more admixtures are used, all shall be compatible.
23
24 In determining total quantity of free water per cubic yard of concrete, liquid
25 admixture that requires dosage greater than 1/2 gallon per cubic yard shall be
26 considered to be free water.

32 (B) Admixture Acceptance. The Engineer’s written acceptance is
33 required prior to use of admixture in concrete. Acceptance is contingent
34 upon furnishing of certified test results by the Contractor and additional tests
35 the Engineer may choose to perform. If the Engineer requests samples of
36 admixture for testing, samples shall be submitted at least 30 days prior to
37 intended use.
38
39 If previously accepted admixture is proposed for use, the Contractor
40 shall provide a certificate of compliance from manufacturer affirming that both
41 admixtures are identical. The Engineer reserves the right to sample
42 admixture even with the certification.
43
44 If mineral admixture is delivered directly to work site or is to be used in
45 ready-mix concrete or precast concrete product, the Contractor shall submit a
46 certificate of compliance signed by manufacturer or concrete supplier.
47
48 (C) Admixture Usage. If chemical admixture or calcium chloride is
49 indicated for use in the contract documents or ordered in writing by the
50 Engineer, quantity shall be as indicated or ordered. If quantity is not
51 indicated or ordered, manufacturer’s recommendation shall be followed.
52
53 Admixtures allowed are Type A or Type F water-reducing, Type B
54 retarding, and Type D or Type G water-reducing and retarding, in accordance
55 with AASHTO M 194, to economize on cement or facilitate construction.

711.03
57 If air-entraining admixture is specified in the contract documents or
58 ordered in writing by the Engineer, quantity of admixture shall be as required
59 to produce concrete with specified air content, when tested in accordance
60 with AASHTO T 152.
61
62 Air-entraining admixture will be allowed even when not specified or
63 ordered. In such event, air content of concrete shall be 4 percent plus or
64 minus 1 percent.
65
66 Replacement of up to 15 percent by weight of portland cement, other
67 than Type IP, with mineral admixture will be allowed, except when high early
68 strength concrete is specified or mineral admixture is prohibited. Weight of
69 mineral admixture shall be equal to or greater than weight of portland cement
70 replaced. In determining free water for concrete, mineral admixture shall be
71 considered to be cement.
72
73 Same brand and percentage of mineral admixture shall be used for
74 concrete in exposed surface of structures.
