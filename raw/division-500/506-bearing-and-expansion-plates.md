4 506.01 Description. This section describes furnishing and placing bronze and
5 stainless steel bearing and expansion plates.
6
7 506.02 Materials.
8
9 Bronze and Stainless Steel Bearing and Expansion Plate 712.09(D)
10
11 506.03 Construction. Submit shop drawings and mill test reports, and obtain the
12 Engineer’s acceptance of those submittal items before incorporating plates into the
13 work. Set bearing plates in position in accordance with the contract documents and
14 in such a manner that full and uniform bearing is provided over entire contact area.
15 Make provisions to keep plates in correct position during concrete placement and
16 include those provisions in shop drawings.
17
18 506.04 Measurement. Bronze and stainless steel bearing and expansion plates
19 will be paid on a lump sum basis. Measurement for payment will not apply.
20
21 506.05 Payment. The Engineer will pay for the accepted bronze and stainless
22 steel bearing and expansion plates on a contract lump sum basis. Payment will be
23 full compensation for work prescribed in this section and the contract documents.
24
