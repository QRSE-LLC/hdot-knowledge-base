310.05
1 SECTION 310 - BROOMING OFF
2
3
4 310.01 Description. This section describes cleaning existing surface or
5 pavement.
6
7 310.02 Materials. None.
8
9 310.03 Construction. Remove earth, dust and other foreign material, grass and
10 other growth, raveled material, existing raised pavement markers, and traffic tapes
11 from entire surface of work area. Remove grass and other growth from edges of
12 work area and closely crop adjacent growth to prevent interference with subsequent
13 project construction. Dispose of debris resulting from brooming off operations.
14
15 Remove loose material and excess dust using mechanically operated street
16 sweeper or mechanically operated broom, both equipped to contain dust generated
17 from cleaning operations, or vacuum with HEPA filter. If necessary, supplement
18 mechanical cleaning by hand brooming. Clean cracks and joints with compressed
19 air. Complete this work while surface is dry.
20
21 Control dust emissions in accordance with Section 620 - Dust Control.
22
23 310.04 Measurement. The Engineer will not measure brooming off for payment.
24
25 310.05 Payment. The Engineer will not pay for brooming off separately and will
26 consider the cost for brooming off as included in the contract prices for the various
27 contract pay items. The cost is for the work prescribed in this section and the
28 contract documents.
