11 313.03 Construction.
12
13 (A) Site Preparation. Prepare surface in accordance with Section 305 -
14 Aggregate Subbase Course.
15
16 (B) Installation. Unroll geotextiles smoothly on prepared surface in
17 longitudinal direction. Do not drag geotextiles. Remove wrinkles and folds
18 by stretching and anchoring. Overlap geotextiles a minimum of 3 feet at
19 longitudinal and transverse joints, or sew geotextiles with 9-inch overlap at
20 sheet intersections. When subgrade soils have California Bearing Ratio
21 (CBR) of less than 1, use sewn seams.
22
23 (C) Sewn Seams. Comply with manufacturer's recommendations for
24 seam, stitch type, and stitching equipment. Seam strengths for both factory-
25 sewn and field-sewn seams shall be equal to or greater than 90 percent of
26 grab strength as measured in accordance with ASTM D 4632. Unless
27 otherwise indicated in the manufacturer’s recommendations, comply with the
28 following requirements:
29
30 (1) Use seams with two parallel rows of stitching, 1/2 inch plus or
31 minus 1/4 inch apart.
32
33 (2) Sew seams with lock stitch (Federal Stitch Type 401).
34
35 (3) When using flat or prayer seam (Type SSa-2), provide
36 minimum distance of 1-1/2 inches between geotextile edge and stitch
37 line nearest to that edge. For other seam types, provide minimum
38 seam allowance of 1 inch.
39
40 (D) Geotextile Placement. Hold geotextiles in place by pins, staples, or
41 piles of permeable material. On curves, fold or cut geotextiles to conform to
42 curve, with appropriate overlap or seam. Install fold or overlap in direction of
43 permeable material placement.
44
45 (E) Geotextile Exposure Following Placement. Limit time exposure of
46 geotextiles to natural elements, between placement and cover, to a maximum
47 of five days. Construction equipment and vehicular traffic will not be allowed
48 directly on geotextiles.

50 (F) Permeable Material Placement. Deposit permeable material in
51 accordance with Section 306 - Untreated Permeable Base Course.
52
53 (G) Damage Repair. Geotextile will be considered damaged if it is torn or
54 punctured, if overlaps or sewn joints are disturbed, or if there is evidence of
55 subgrade pumping, intrusion, or roadbed distortion. Repair damaged
56 geotextile by removing permeable material around damaged or displaced
57 area and by replacing damaged geotextile with a patch of same type of
58 geotextile. Overlap existing geotextile a minimum of 3 feet from edge of
59 damaged area, or repair damaged sheets by sewing. Replace and compact
60 removed permeable material.

62 313.04 Measurement. The Engineer will not measure permeable separator for
63 payment.
64
65 313.05 Payment. The Engineer will not pay for permeable separator separately
66 and will consider the cost for permeable separator as included in the contract price
67 for the untreated permeable base course pay item. Payment will be full
68 compensation for the work prescribed in this section and the contract documents.
