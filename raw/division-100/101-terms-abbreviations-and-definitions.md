6 101.01 Meaning of Terms. The specifications are generally written in the
7 imperative mood. In sentences using the imperative mood, the subject, “the
8 Contractor shall”, is implied. In the material specifications, the subject may
9 also be the supplier, fabricator, or manufacturer supplying material, products,
10 or equipment for use on the project. The word “will” generally pertains to
11 decisions or actions of the State.
12
13 When a publication is specified, it refers to the most recent date of issue,
14 including interim publications, before the bid opening date for the project, unless
15 a specific date or year of issue is provided.

192 in the work, contract time or contract price. The purposes of a change order
