17 650.03 Construction. Saw cut and remove existing curb, gutter, and sidewalk in
18 accordance with Subsection 202.03(B) - Removal of Concrete Structures.
19 Construct curb ramps in accordance with Section 634 - Portland Cement
20 Concrete Sidewalks. Reinforce with 6 x 6 - W1.4 x W1.4 welded wire fabric, unless
21 otherwise indicated in the contract documents. Provide construction joints where
22 curb ramps join existing or new sidewalks, and where curb ramp aprons abut drop
23 curbs or gutters.
24
25 Construct drop curbs in accordance with Section 638 – Portland Cement
26 Concrete Curb and Gutter. Match face of drop curbs to face of abutting curbs.
27
28 Detectable warnings shall be 24 inches in the direction of travel and extend
29 the full width of the curb ramp or flush surface. Truncated domes shall have a
30 diameter of 0.9 inch at the bottom, a diameter of 0.4 inch at the top, a height of 0.2
31 inch and a center to center spacing of 2.35 inches measured along one side of a
32 square arrangement. Domes shall be aligned on a square grid in the predominant
33 direction of travel to permit wheels to roll between the domes. There shall be a
34 minimum of 70 percent contract in light reflectance between the detectable warning
35 and an adjoining surface, or the detectable warning shall be ‘safety yellow’. The
36 material used to provide visual contrast shall be an integral part of the detectable
37 warning surface. The detectable warning shall be located so that the edge
38 nearest the curb line or other potential hazard is 6 to 8 inches from the curb line or
39 other potential hazard.
