635.05
1 SECTION 635 – HOT MIX ASPHALT (HMA) SIDEWALKS
2 635.01 Description. This section describes constructing HMA sidewalks.
3 635.02 Materials.
4 HMA Pavement Mix No. V 401.02
5 Aggregate for Untreated Base 703.06
6 Increase asphalt content for HMA Pavement Mix No. V at least 0.5 percent
7 above that used for HMA pavements.
8 635.03 Construction.
9 (A) Excavating. Excavate to required depth, remove unsuitable
10 material, and compact subgrade to firm, even surface in accordance with
11 Subsection 203.03(A)(4) - Subexcavation.
12 (B) Forms. Use full-depth wood or metal forms that are straight, free of
13 warp and of sufficient strength to resist pressure of HMA without springing.
14 Brace and stake forms to remain in both horizontal and vertical alignment
15 until removal.
16 (C) Bed Course. Use aggregate for untreated base, as bed course
17 material. Place bed course material a minimum of four inches in depth.
18 Compact with three passes of lightweight mechanical tamper, roller, or
19 vibratory system.
20 (D) Placing HMA. Place HMA on compacted bed course to the required
21 depth. Compact HMA with hand operated or power roller. Hand tamp areas
22 inaccessible to roller. Compact asphalt to 90 percent of maximum theoretical
23 density in accordance with AASHTO T 209.
24 (E) Finishing. Finish sidewalk as plane surface with two percent
25 maximum cross slope towards roadway.
26 (F) Backfilling. After sidewalk has set, fill remaining excavated areas
27 with material acceptable to the Engineer. Place and tamp material,
28 acceptable to the Engineer, in layers up to six inches in depth.
29 635.04 Measurement. HMA sidewalks will be paid on a lump sum basis.
30 Measurement for payment will not apply.
31 635.05 Payment. The Engineer will pay for accepted HMA sidewalk on a
32 contract lump sum basis. Payment will be full compensation for work prescribed in
33 this section and contract documents.
