4 692.01 Description. This section describes voluntary partnering and applies
5 to non-resurfacing projects with a construction cost estimated at over $10 million.
6
7 692.02 Materials. Not applicable.
8
9 692.03 Construction. Department intends to encourage foundation of
10 cohesive partnership with Contractor and its principal subcontractors and
11 suppliers. Each organization will structure this partnership to draw on strengths
12 and to identify and achieve reciprocal goals. Objectives are effective and
13 efficient contract performance and completion within budget, on schedule, and in
14 accordance with contract documents.
15
16 Partnership will be bilateral in makeup and participation will be voluntary.
17 Both parties will agree and share equally in costs associated with effectuating
18 this partnering.
19
20 Contractor's management personnel and Highways Division's
21 Construction and Maintenance Branch (HWY-C) or District Office will initiate
22 partnering before start of work and before preconstruction conference. Initiate a
23 partnering development seminar/team building workshop. Department's District
24 Construction Engineer and Contractor's authorized representative will be
25 coordinators in determining attendees, agenda, location, and duration of
26 workshop.
