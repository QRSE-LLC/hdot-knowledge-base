27 654.03 Construction.
28
29 (A) Restrainer Units. Grease restrainer cables or steel rods with
30 corrosion inhibiting grease. Provide sheathing to protect corrosion-inhibiting
31 grease as necessary. Install restrainer units as shown in contract
32 documents.
33
34 (B) Retrofit Work. If needed, additional as-built plans for existing
35 structures may be obtained from the Highway Design Section, Design
36 Branch, Highways Division, Department of Transportation, 601 Kamokila
37 Boulevard, Room 609, Kapolei, HI 96707; telephone number: (808) 692-
38 7586.
39
40 (1) Fabrication and Installation. Drill hole in existing concrete
41 and install steel plate assemblies at locations shown in contract
42 documents. Chip out existing concrete to required radius around
43 corner of drilled hole and provide smooth, curved surface for cable
44 entry or exit. Chip around any exposed reinforcing steel and restore
45 reinforcing steel to existing cover. Connect restrainer units through
46 bridge girders as shown in contract documents. Loosen hex nut to

48 restrainer cable.

50 (2) Inspection. At least one day before restoring concrete, notify
51 Engineer that restrainer cables are in position and ready for
52 inspection.
