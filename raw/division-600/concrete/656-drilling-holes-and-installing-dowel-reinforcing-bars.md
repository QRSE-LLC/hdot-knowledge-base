5 656.01 Description. This section describes drilling holes and installing dowel
6 reinforcing bars where new concrete is to be joined to existing concrete by means of
7 dowel reinforcing bars grouted into holes drilled into existing concrete.
8
9 656.02 Materials.
10
11 Reinforcing Steel 602
12
13 Epoxy-Resin Adhesive 712.02
14
15 Epoxy Grout 712.04(B)
16
17 Submit test certificates indicating conformance of materials to standards
18 referenced in this subsection.
19
20 656.03 Construction.
21
22 (A) Drilling and Installation. Drill holes by methods that will not shatter
23 or damage concrete adjacent to hole. Unless otherwise specified or shown
24 on plans, diameter of drilled holes shall be 1/2-inch larger than nominal dowel
25 diameter. Holes encountering longitudinal or transverse reinforcement during
26 drilling will be rejected. Adjacent to rejected hole, drill new hole to required
27 depth, avoiding existing reinforcement. Place dowel reinforcing bars after
28 drilling to depth shown on plans and cleaning holes of dust and residue. Fill
29 holes with epoxy grout after removing fine particles.
30
31 (B) Inspection. Before filling holes with epoxy grout, notify the Engineer
32 that holes are ready for inspection.
33
34 656.04 Measurement. The Engineer will measure drilled holes and installed
35 dowel reinforcing bars per each in accordance with contract documents.
