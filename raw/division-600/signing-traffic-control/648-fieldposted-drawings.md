drawings in accordance with the contract documents.
648.02 Materials. Not applicable.

648.05
Basis of Payment. The Engineer will pay for the accepted field-posted
drawings on a lump sum basis.
Payment will be full compensation for the work
prescribed in this section and the contract documents.
