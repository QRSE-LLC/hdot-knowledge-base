1 SECTION 611 - HAND-LAID RIPRAP
2
3
4 611.01 Description. This section describes constructing hand-laid riprap.
5
6 611.02 Materials.
7
8 Geotextile 716.02
9
10 Stones for riprap shall be clean, sound, durable, one-man stone. Stones
11 shall be at least 3 inches thick in least dimension containing more than half cubic
12 foot in volume. Stones that do not extend through revetment will not be allowed.
13
14 611.03 Construction. Excavate in accordance with Section 206 – Excavation
15 and Backfill for Drainage Facilities.
16
17 Make sufficient excavation to expose foundation bed. Free foundation bed of
18 brush, trees, stumps, roots, debris, and other objectionable materials, and dress to
19 smooth surface. Prior to placing geotextile fabric provide 3 days notice to Engineer
20 for inspection of foundation.
21
22 After foundation is acceptable to Engineer, place geotextile fabric in
23 accordance with manufacturer’s recommendations or as indicated in contract
24 documents. Remove and replace geotextile fabric that is displaced or damaged
25 during riprap placement, at no increase in contract cost or contract time. Distribute
26 stones to prevent large accumulations of either large or small sized stones.
27
28 611.04 Measurement. Hand-laid riprap will be paid on a lump sum basis.
29 Measurement for payment will not apply.
30
31 611.05 Payment. Engineer will pay for the accepted quantities of hand-laid riprap
32 on a contract lump sum basis. Payment will be full compensation for the work
33 prescribed in this section and the contract documents.
34
35 Engineer will pay for the following pay item when included in proposal
36 schedule:
37
38 Pay Item Pay Unit
39
40 Hand-Laid Riprap Lump Sum
41
42 Lump sum monthly payment basis will be determined in accordance with
43 Subsection 109.01 – Schedule of Agreed Prices for Lump Sum Price Items.
