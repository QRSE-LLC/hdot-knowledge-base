SECTION 637 - SLOTTED DRAINS
637.01
637.02 Materials.
Structure Backfill Material
Description. This section describes furnishing and placing slotted drains.

Class C concrete, used for plugs at open ends of slotted drain pipes, shall
conform to Section 601 - Structural Concrete.
Bolts, nuts, angles, and pipe spacers shall be zinc-coated in accordance with
ASTM A 153. Bolts shall be steel machined bolts with cut threads and American
Standard Regular hexagon heads. Nuts shall be American Standard Regular
cold-punched hexagon nuts. Bolt material shall conform to ASTM A 307, Grade A.
Nut material shall conform to ASTM A 563, Grade A. Pipe spacers shall conform to
ASTM A 283, Grade D.
637.03 Construction. Excavate trenches to dimensions and grades in
accordance with Section 206 - Excavation and Backfill for Drainage Facilities and
Section 603 - Culverts and Storm Drains.
Install slotted drains.
Place slotted drains to line and grade indicated in contract documents,
adequately supported, until adjacent construction is completed. Provide temporary
covers, as required, to prevent foreign material from entering slotted drains during
construction.
637.04
Measurement.
Slotted drains will be paid on a lump sum basis.
Measurement for payment will not apply.
637.05
Payment. Engineer will pay for accepted slotted drains on a contract
lump sum basis. Payment will be full compensation for work prescribed in this
section and contract documents.
