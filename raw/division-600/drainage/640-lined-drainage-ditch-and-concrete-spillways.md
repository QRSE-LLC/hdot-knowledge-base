SECTION 640 - LINED DRAINAGE DITCH AND CONCRETE SPILLWAYS
640.01 Description. This section describes constructing lined drainage ditches
and spillways.
640.02 Materials.
Concrete
601
Shotcrete
628
Reinforcing Steel
709.01
Curing Materials
711.01
Class A or Class B concrete, used for lined drainage ditches and spillways,
shall conform to Section 601 - Structural Concrete.
640.03
Construction.
(A)
Excavation.
Excavation. Excavate according to Section 207 - Ditch and Channel
(B)
Placing of Concrete and Shotcrete. Prior to placing concrete or
shotcrete, provide 2 working days notice to Engineer for inspection of
reinforcing steel and prepared surface.
Place concrete or shotcrete only
after prepared surfaces and placement of reinforcing steel is acceptable to
Engineer. Materials shall be as specified in contract documents.
Place and cure concrete and shotcrete according to Section 503 -
Concrete Structures and Section 628 - Shotcrete, respectively.
Chamfer exposed edges 3/4 inch.
640.04
Measurement.
(A)
(B)
When spillway is paid for per linear foot, Engineer will measure
spillway per linear foot in accordance with contract documents. When
spillway is paid on a lump sum basis, measurement for payment will
not apply.
Lined drainage ditch will be paid on a lump sum basis. Measurement
for payment will not apply.
