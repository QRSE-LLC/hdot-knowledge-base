4 616.01 Description. This section describes constructing permanent irrigation
5 system, including pipes, sprinkler heads, drip emitters, valves, automatic controllers,
6 remote control valves, power and control wiring, and related equipment and
7 materials.

174 (6) Remote Control Valve. Electric control valve in specified size

178 Remote control valve shall have integrated union in discharge side,

223 working pressure of 150 pounds per square inch. Regulator pressure shall
224 be set between 30 and 40 pounds per square inch.
