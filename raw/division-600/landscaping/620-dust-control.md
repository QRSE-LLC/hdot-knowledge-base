620.03
1 SECTION 620 – DUST CONTROL
2
3
4 620.01 Description. This section describes providing dust control to mitigate
5 project dust nuisance.
6
7 620.02 Materials. Geobinder shall readily mix with water to form thin protective
8 crust that will resist effects of water and wind erosion. Geobinder shall be
9 environmentally benign, harmless to fish, birds, plants, and animals, and shall be
10 nontoxic and noncombustible.
11
12 Surfactant shall enable water to be absorbed by soil more effectively by
13 modifying surface tension of water.
14
15 620.03 Construction.
16
17 (A) Preconstruction Requirements. Submit plan outlining design and
18 construction methods for project dust control. Design to minimize project
19 impacts on adjacent properties. Include details for furnishing and applying
20 water and constructing and maintaining dust screens. Provide water pollution
21 control measures that are for dust control and that meet requirements of
22 Section 209 - Temporary Water Pollution, Dust, and Erosion Control.
23
24 Dust control plan shall contain the following:
25
26 (1) List of dust producing activities.
27
28 (2) Method(s) that shall be used to mitigate or eliminate amount of
29 dust produced, such as spraying water from water truck, using
30 misters, chemical dust controlling agents, or combination thereof;
31 hydromulching, keeping soil moist, and grassing.
32
33 (3) Name of designated Contractor’s representative responsible for
34 implementing dust control plan, representative’s home, business, and
35 fax number. Representative shall have authority to resolve complaints
36 and inquiries. The Department will send public complaints and
37 inquiries regarding dust from construction activities to the
38 representative.
39
40 (4) Sampling program to collect data on effectiveness of methods
41 used in controlling dust performed by independent certified monitoring
42 laboratory. Direct independent certified monitoring laboratory to begin
43 monitoring for dust before construction begins to establish base data.
44
45 (5) Proactive community relations program regarding dust issues.

620.04
47 (B) Construction Requirements.
48
49 (1) Do not begin work on the project until complete submittals
50 detailed in Subsection 620.03(A) - Preconstruction Requirements
51 have been accepted by the Engineer.
52
53 (2) Conduct proactive community relations program regarding dust
54 issues.
55
56 (3) Maintain dust screens until permanent ground cover has been
57 established. Revise dust screen installations, as necessary, to
58 complete work and to meet environmental and climate changes.
59
60 (4) Apply water uniformly by pressure-type tank truck equipped
61 with spray system and adequate control apparatus. Ensure uniform
62 application of water. Use watering systems such as pipe, hose, and
63 spray apparatus, only if uniform application of water can be ensured.
64
65 (5) Apply water as conditions require. Prevent water from wetting
66 vehicles, pedestrians, and existing pavements. Repair or compensate
67 for damages caused by watering.
68
69 (6) Geobinders with surfactants may be used to minimize water
70 consumption.
71
72 (7) Employ best management practices (BMP's) with regard to
73 dust control water leaving project site. Washing down of debris or dirt
74 into drainage or sewer systems, or State waters will not be allowed.
75
76 (8) Modify and resubmit plans and construction schedules to
77 correct conditions that develop during construction.
78
79 (9) Correct deficiencies in dust control methods based on
80 recommendations by independent certified monitoring laboratory.
81 Implement corrected methods to dust control plan within 24 hours.
82
83 (10) Continue monitoring for dust until at least 60 days after final
84 project acceptance.
85
86 (11) Failure to lessen dust nuisance shall result in work suspension.
87 The Engineer may elect to have work done by others and deduct the
88 cost from payments due to the Contractor. The Engineer may elect to
89 continue or to increase deduction of retainage from progress payment.
90
91 (12) Coordinate with and pay for fines imposed by the Department
92 of Health.
93
94 620.04 Measurement. The Engineer will not measure dust control for payment.

96 620.05 Payment. The Engineer will not pay for dust control separately and will
97 consider the cost for dust control as included in the contract lump sum price under
98 Section 209 - Temporary Water Pollution, Dust, and Erosion Control. The cost is for
99 the work prescribed in this section and the contract documents.
100
101 The Engineer will not pay for work to repair or to compensate for damages or
102 fines caused by dust or water.
