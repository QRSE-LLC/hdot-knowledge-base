299 (1) Mechanical Joints. Clean bell and spigot end of pipe and
300 rubber gasket before assembly. Place gland, followed by gasket, over
301 spigot end of pipe that is inserted into bell. Face small side of gasket
302 and lip side of gland towards bell. Push gasket into position so that
303 gasket seats evenly in bell as gland is moved against face of gasket.
304

306 assembly.

311 wrench acceptable to the Engineer, beginning at bottom, then top and
312 so on. Conform normal range of bolt torques for standard cast iron
