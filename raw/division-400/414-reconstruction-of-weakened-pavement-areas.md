1 SECTION 414 - RECONSTRUCTION OF WEAKENED PAVEMENT AREAS
2
3
4 414.01 Description. This section describes reconstructing weakened pavement
5 areas of the existing roadway.
6
7 414.02 Materials.
8
9 Hot Mix Glassphalt Base Course 312.02
10
11 414.03 Construction. Saw cut and excavate weakened pavement areas as
12 indicated in the contract documents. Reconstructed area boundaries shall have
13 square vertical faces after saw cutting. Prepare bottom of excavation in accordance
14 with Subsection 203.03(D) - Subgrade Preparation.
15
16 Backfill excavated areas to existing roadway grade with hot mix glassphalt
17 base (HMGB) course in accordance with Section 312 - Hot Mix Glassphalt Base
18 Course.
19
20 If unsuitable material is encountered at bottom of specified excavation,
21 remove unsuitable material, backfill, and compact in accordance with Subsection
22 304 - Aggregate Base Course or Subsection 305 - Aggregate Subbase Course.
23 Dispose of excavated material in accordance with Subsection 201.03(F) - Removal
24 and Disposal of Material.
25
26 Complete backfilling of excavated areas before end of workday.
27
28 414.04 Measurement. The Engineer will measure excavation of weakened
29 pavement areas per cubic yard in accordance with the contract documents.
30
31 414.05 Payment. The Engineer will pay for the accepted excavation of
32 weakened pavement areas at the contract price per cubic yard. Payment will be full
33 compensation for the work prescribed in this section and the contract documents.
34
